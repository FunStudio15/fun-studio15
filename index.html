About

Fun_Studio15 is an indie studio making games and music.

Fun_Studio15 was created in 2024. We started with games on popular platforms such as Roblox© where we started with simple games and worked our way up to Open-World and Story games but at the middle of 2025 we made a change and started using the Unity Engine© which we are now actively using and are doing our best to release at least 2 unique titles in 2027.
